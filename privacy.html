JAHANTI / COMMUNITY

Privacy Policy

JahAnti is an independent 16+ gaming community. Information on this page is for visitors and members.

Who manages the information?

JahAnti is an independent, currently non-commercial gaming community for players aged 16 and older. The JahAnti community administrators manage the data published on this website. For privacy requests, contact our administrators using the contact page. We intend to publish a dedicated privacy email and fuller operator details once they are available.

What information is used?

For approved public members, JahAnti may display a community username, games played, confirmed match outcomes, standings, tournament participation and achievements. A player may also provide an optional profile photo, short biography, gaming handles and social-media links. The private administration spreadsheet contains internal permanent player IDs and administrative audit information.

Why is this information used?

JahAnti uses these details to run community tournaments, keep accurate scoreboards, recognize accomplishments and help members find each other. Publishing a personal profile requires the appropriate permission or other lawful basis; players are informed about public rankings before they join. Optional photos and social links are published only when approved.

Who can see it?

Public profile pages and published ranking results can be viewed by anyone online and may be indexed or shared. Administrators manage the non-public database. Some hosting and linked platforms, including GitHub Pages and social networks opened by users, handle technical information under their own policies.

How long is it kept?

We keep community information for as long as needed to run the community, honor valid privacy requests and maintain justified historical tournament records. Optional public profile details can be corrected or removed on request. Removal of an entire historical match may require assessment because other participants' records can be affected. We review the necessity of retained data when participation ends.

Your choices and rights

You may request access, correction, restriction or erasure of eligible personal information, object to relevant processing, or withdraw consent where consent applies. A request does not automatically require deleting legitimate tournament history in every circumstance. Contact administrators using Contact. You can also complain to the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Cookies and future analytics

We are not currently planning advertising or Google Analytics. If we later introduce tracking or advertising technologies, we will update this notice and implement required consent choices before enabling them. Our embedded or linked third-party services may have their own privacy policies.

Updates

This notice is an initial community privacy statement. We may update it when JahAnti features or the data it processes change.
