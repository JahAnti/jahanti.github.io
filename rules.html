Community Rules

JahAnti is an independent 16+ gaming community. Information on this page is for visitors and members.

Respect the crew

JahAnti is intended for players aged 16 and older. Treat all players respectfully. Harassment, discrimination, threats, impersonation and unwanted publication of others' private information are not acceptable.

Fair competition

Do not cheat, exploit bugs, falsify scores or deliberately sabotage tournaments. Follow the rules for each EA FC, shooter or Domino competition, including administrator instructions, deadlines and result confirmation.

Match reporting

Report results accurately to an authorized administrator. Administrators may use clearly identified walkovers or administrative results where tournament rules permit. Simulated results must not be presented as genuine played matches.

Your profile

Ask an administrator before publishing somebody else's profile picture, links or identifying details. You may request edits to your own optional public information. Community names must not impersonate others.

Community moderation

Administrators may review reported misconduct and apply appropriate actions, including warnings, tournament restrictions or removal from the community. Contact administrators if you disagree with a decision.
